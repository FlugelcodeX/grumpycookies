# Grumpy Cookies 🍪

> A custom website developed for a cookie business to improve its online presence, product presentation, and customer ordering experience.

## 📌 Project Overview

**Grumpy Cookies** is a client-focused web development project created to help a local cookie business establish a stronger online presence.

The client needed a website where customers could easily discover their products, learn about the brand, and submit requests for customized orders such as birthday cakes.

I designed and developed the website around the client's needs, focusing on a simple, visually appealing experience that represents the personality of the brand while making important customer actions easier to find.

## 🎯 The Problem

The client faced several challenges:

- Limited online presence for showcasing their products
- Customers had difficulty discovering available products and services
- Custom orders required a more organized way to collect customer requests
- The brand needed a website that better represented its personality
- Important information needed to be presented in a simple and accessible way

The goal was not simply to create a website, but to create a **digital storefront that could help the business communicate with customers more effectively.**

## 💡 The Solution

I created a responsive website centered around the client's brand and customer journey.

The website provides:

- A dedicated landing page for the brand
- Clear product presentation
- Custom order/request functionality
- Birthday cake request form
- Easy-to-follow navigation
- Responsive layouts for mobile and desktop
- A playful visual identity matching the brand

The interface was designed to keep the experience straightforward while giving the products and brand personality more attention.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🍪 Product Showcase
Products are presented in a visually engaging way so customers can quickly understand what the business offers.

### 🎂 Custom Order Request
A dedicated request form allows customers to provide information for customized orders, including birthday cake requests.

### 📱 Responsive Design
The website adapts to different screen sizes, allowing customers to browse from desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

### 🎨 Brand-focused UI/UX
The visual design was created around the personality of **Grumpy Cookies**, rather than using a generic restaurant template.

### 🧭 Simplified Navigation
Important sections and customer actions are organized to reduce unnecessary steps when browsing the website.
